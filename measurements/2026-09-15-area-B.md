## Comparison with SWAG / LIBRARY.md

- Everything measured is far below the per-family SWAG: pm-lib total ~2.4k LUT4 (RegFile is the superset), Video 126 LUT4 + 5 BRAM vs 3k/16 KB, Keyer 379 vs 1k, IRIG+GPS ~600 vs 1.5k, H2 core 1.1k vs 3k (SoC not measured). No block is oversized. No block uses BRAM except pm_video (5 DP16KD, matches LIBRARY.md).
- DSP inference is the anomaly, not LUTs: Zones (6), RegFile (6), Keyer (5), IRIG (5), Wspr (2), Video (2) all pull MULT18X18D for what should be counters/compares. Cheap on the 85F (156 DSP) but worth a `-fclash-...`/explicit-shift look if the DSP budget ever matters.
- Fmax: five tops miss 100 MHz. Matrix/RegFile (58-60 MHz) matter because RegFile sits on the H2 bus at the system clock; the fix is registering the `kIx`-indexed `map_out` Vec fold. GPS/Keyer/IRIG are 25 MHz-domain designs and pass their own clocks. H2 core at 68.7 MHz is the CPU's single-cycle ALU+stack path; SoC-level Fmax still unmeasured.

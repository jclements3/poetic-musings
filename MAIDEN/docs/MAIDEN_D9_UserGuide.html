User Guide and Release Notes

Everything needed to run a MAIDEN session at the field and read the report. Written for the project owner now; intended to be handed to a club volunteer later. Design detail is in D6 Design; this page only says what to do.

Session workflow

1 · Deploy

  1. Station A at the pilot station, lens toward the pattern-box center. Station B 75 m down the flight line (paced marks on the fence), lens toward the same aerial volume. Station C near the active threshold, looking down the centerline.
  2. Level each tripod; plug battery; confirm the status LED shows PPS lock within 2 min.

2 · Survey

  1. Start a 5-minute GNSS average at each station (button on case; LED blinks during average).
  2. Sight the far landmark through the rail; read heading; enter on the station keypad or host app.

3 · Calibrate

  1. Hold the checkerboard at ~8 m in front of each camera; walk the 12 poses shown on the card; host app reports reprojection error — redo if > 0.5 px.
  2. Walk the corner reflector across each radar beam at ~20 m; host app shows the peak.

4 · Record

  1. Press Record on the host app (all three stations start together).
  2. Clap once in front of Station A.

5 · Fly

Nothing changes for the pilot. One aircraft in the box at a time for scored flights.

6 · Pull and report

  1. Stop recording; pull the three SD cards (and the airborne card on validation flights).
  2. Insert into the host; maiden run --session today. The app ingests, fuses, recognizes, scores, and writes a PDF per flight.
  3. Hand the pilot the PDF or send it to them.

Reading the pilot report

Scores table

One row per recognized maneuver, 0–10 with a ± band. Deductions listed in plain language: "loop 4 m narrower at the top than the bottom", "roll exited 6° right of entry heading."

Geometry overlays

Flown path (solid) over the ideal figure (dashed) from the judge's view. Gray band is the position confidence band.

Approach panel

Glideslope angle, speed over threshold, touchdown distance from the target, and a scatter of the session's landings.

Field-rule flags

Any flight-line or no-fly-zone crossing with time and a map mark. Informational; MAIDEN does not adjudicate.

Bands matter. Bands come from the validation campaign (D8 Validation Report). A score with a wide band means MAIDEN saw the maneuver poorly (sun, distance, drop-out), not that the pilot flew it poorly.

Troubleshooting

SymptomLikely causeAction
Status LED never shows PPS lockSky view blocked; GNSS antenna cableMove station 2 m; check cable; do not record without lock
Report says "degraded sync"PPS lost mid-session; clap used as fallbackScores still valid; position bands widened; check Ch 6 log
Maneuver missing from scores tableTracker lost the aircraft (sun, distance)Check overlay for gaps; re-aim B toward the box center next session
Approach speed blankStation C radar saw no returnConfirm C is on the active threshold; reflector check
Report takes > 10 minHost busy with video encodingUse the host's SSD; close other apps

Consent and data

  • Ask before recording anyone else's flight; the one-paragraph consent card is in the case lid.
  • The pilot owns their report and clips. Raw session data stays on the project archive for validation only.
  • No live video is streamed; stations record locally.

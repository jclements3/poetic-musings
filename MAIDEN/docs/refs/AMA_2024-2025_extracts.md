# AMA RC Aerobatics 2024–2025 — extracts relevant to MAIDEN scoring

Source: `AMA_RC_Aerobatics_2024-2025.pdf` (this directory), fetched
2026-08-21 from modelaircraft.org (Competition → Rulebooks). Quotes are
verbatim from the PDF text layer; section numbers are the rulebook's.

## §15 (preamble) — the maneuvering area and centering

> "bounded by lines 60 degrees each side of center. The vertical height
> shall not exceed 60 degrees from the horizontal. […] vertical poles at
> the center position and 60 degrees right and left on a line
> approximately 150 meters in front of the contestant."

> "Center maneuvers should be performed centered in the maneuvering
> area in a plane exactly perpendicular to the judges' line of sight to
> the model. Scored turnaround maneuvers should not exceed the 60 degree
> right and left limits of the maneuvering area."

> "Maneuvers should be performed along a line of flight approximately
> 150 to 175 meters from the judges […] Infractions of any of the above
> rules are cause for downgrading in addition to those downgrades listed
> in the Description of Maneuvers section."

> "lines into and out of maneuvers are part of the maneuver and are
> always judged"

## §15.6 — call deductions

> "Failure to call the initiation of the takeoff and landing or box
> entry will result in a one (1) point deduction for the upcoming
> maneuver."

## §18.7 — the judge protocol MAIDEN's campaign mirrors (D7)

> "Each flight should be judged by at least two (2) judges, with their
> scores averaged or totaled to give a final score for the flight. Each
> maneuver will be scored immediately after it is performed. Judges
> shall score maneuvers individually and without consultation between
> them."

## §19 — the downgrade authority

> "Judging for AMA and F3A Aerobatic maneuvers will be performed
> according to the F3 R/C Aerobatic Aircraft Manoeuvre Execution Guide,
> as found in Volume F3 Radio Controlled Aerobatics, of the FAI Sporting
> Code."

## Definitions (rulebook glossary)

> "Track: The trajectory or flight path of the center of gravity of the
> model with respect to fixed ground reference."

> "Symmetry: The balanced and equal correspondence of opposing or
> superimposed maneuver elements with respect to size, shape, and
> position."

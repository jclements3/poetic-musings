Architecture Description

Three identical ground stations record video, CW Doppler radar, and IRIG-B time into IRIG-106 Chapter 10 files. A processing host fuses them into a 3D track, recognizes maneuvers, and scores them. An airborne 6-DOF logger provides truth during validation only. Requirements satisfied by each element are cited inline; the requirements themselves live in D2 SyRS + RTM.

Fusion concept and expected performance FIGURE 2

Angle-only triangulation from A and B fixes position; three radial velocities from the Doppler chains resolve the full velocity vector without differentiating noisy positions. Error scales as R²/B, so baseline and survey accuracy (GS-004, GS-005) matter more than camera resolution. A timing error of 1/30 s at 30 m/s is 1 m — the same as GPS — which is why SYS-006 demands end-to-end IRIG-B discipline.

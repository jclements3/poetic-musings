Verification & Validation Plan

How MAIDEN proves it meets D2 SyRS + RTM. Bench and HIL tests retire hardware and software requirements one at a time; the validation campaign compares the fused track to 6-DOF truth and is the gate between Phase 1 and Phase 2. Results are recorded in D8 Validation Report.

Verification approach

  • Verification (did we build it right): each requirement has one test below with a measurable pass criterion.
  • Validation (did we build the right thing): the campaign in §3 and the judge correlation, which truth data cannot supply.
  • Every test records raw .ch10 plus a one-page result sheet committed with the release.
  • Software tests run in CI on every commit against HIL and twin data (SW-005).

Verification against 6-DOF truth FIGURE

  1. Time-align all Ch. 10 streams on the common IRIG-B base; confirm with a known event visible to all sources.
  2. Transform the logger's GNSS/IMU solution into the surveyed field ENU frame.
  3. Compute per-sample residuals: position, velocity, and attitude where the fused track estimates it.
  4. Roll up per flight and per maneuver type: RMS, 95th percentile, track continuity.
  5. Gate: VT-10, VT-11, VT-12 pass on ≥ 8 of 10 flights before Phase 2 begins.
  6. Carry the residual statistics forward as confidence bands in un-instrumented reports.
  7. Separately, correlate MAIDEN scores with judge scores on the same flights (VT-21).

Validation campaign PHASE 1 · NOV–DEC 2026

AircraftMy own: larger sport/pattern airframe (RTK-capable logger) and a smaller pattern ship (≤ 60 g logger)Flights≥ 10, over at least three weekend sessionsContentFull Sportsman sequence × 6; touch-and-go series × 2; safety-rule edge cases × 2 (flown at a safe margin)ConditionsDaylight, wind ≤ 15 kt; at least one session with low sun to stress the trackerJudgesTwo club Pattern judges score the Sportsman flights blind from Station A videoOutputsRaw .ch10 per station and aircraft; residual tables; judge sheets

Flight card (per flight)

  1. Stations deployed, surveyed, recording; PPS lock on all three (Ch 6).
  2. Logger armed; GPS fix; clap in front of Station A.
  3. Takeoff; sequence per card; land.
  4. Pull airborne SD; note wind, sun, anomalies.
  5. Same evening: convert, fuse, residuals, append to campaign table.

Concept of Operations

MAIDEN gives pilots at Rocket City Radio Controllers objective, same-day feedback on their flying: where the airplane actually went, how fast it crossed the threshold, how round the loop was, and where it landed — without requiring any electronics on the aircraft.

Need

Today a pilot learns from an instructor's eye or a judge's score sheet, both of which are subjective and arrive with limited detail. Pattern pilots practice without a judge most of the time; students get "a little high on final" without a number. MAIDEN replaces guesswork with a measured trajectory and a rubric-based score. See D2 SyRS + RTM for the requirements this drives.

Operating site RCRC · 4100 LEEMAN FERRY RD · 34.685, -86.592

Station A sits at the pilot/judge position and looks into the pattern box. Station B is 50–100 m down the flight line and shares A's aerial volume for triangulation. Station C is near a runway threshold for approach radar. Site coordinates come from the Phase 0 survey and are recorded in each station's TMATS record (D4 ICD).

Operational scenarios

S1 — Pattern practice session (primary)

  1. Deploy three stations, survey, start Ch. 10 recording (≤ 15 min, one person; procedure in D9 User Guide).
  2. Pilot flies a Sportsman sequence; MAIDEN records continuously.
  3. Pull station SD cards; ingest; fusion; maneuver recognition; scoring.
  4. Pilot receives a one-page report within 10 minutes: per-maneuver score, overlay of flown vs. ideal geometry, top three deductions in plain language.

S2 — Student approach and landing

Same deployment. Report shows glideslope angle, speed over threshold, touchdown distance from target, and a scatter of the session's landings.

S3 — Validation flight (development only)

My own aircraft carries the 6-DOF truth logger. After the flight, the airborne log is converted to Ch. 10 and compared to the fused track per D7 V&V Plan. This is how MAIDEN earns the right to score aircraft that carry nothing.

S4 — Club demo (Feb 2027)

Saturday session: deploy, record several members' flights with consent, hand out reports, collect feedback. Exit criteria in D7 V&V Plan.

Constraints and assumptions

  • Single aircraft in the pattern box at a time; multiple aircraft is a Phase 2 hardening item.
  • Daylight, VFR, wind ≤ 15 kt for scored sessions.
  • Recording other members requires their consent; reports are theirs.
  • Unlicensed 10.5 / 24 GHz CW modules at Part 15 power; no transmit license needed.
  • Nothing on the aircraft is required for scoring once validation passes (SYS-001).
